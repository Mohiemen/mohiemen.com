THE PROBLEM

Manufacturers run on manual systems that don't scale

Across every factory I've worked in — spinning, dyeing, denim, knits — the same gap shows up: production status, quality data and sustainability metrics live in disconnected spreadsheets, paper logs and someone's memory. Problems surface days after they start, on incomplete information, because nobody has one place to look.

THE PLATFORM

What Intelactory actually does

AI CONTROL TOWER

See risk before it costs an order

Watches every order, line and lot at once, flags delays, capacity conflicts and shortages while there's still time to act, and proposes the fix alongside the warning.

TRACEABILITY ENGINE

Fibre-to-carton genealogy

A complete lot and batch genealogy graph across the operation — trace a defect back to its dye batch, or forward to every shipment it touched, in one click.

TNA ENGINE

Time & action, protected automatically

Milestone tracking that catches a slipping delivery date before the milestone actually breaks, instead of after.

DIGITAL PRODUCT PASSPORTS

Compliance as a by-product, not a scramble

Builds the product passport as the order is made, so audit and buyer compliance comes out of running the factory rather than a pre-shipment fire drill.

SUSTAINABILITY & ESG

Chemical, water & energy reporting

Built alongside live ZDHC and AFD-funded compliance work, so the reporting layer matches what factories are already being asked to track.

Purpose-built today for spinning, knitting, weaving, dyeing, washing, printing, garments and composite mills — see the full platform at intelactory.com ↗.

WHY ME

I'm not approaching this from the outside

I've spent eleven years on the floor and in the planning office — as a production planner, an industrial engineer, and now the person coordinating 20+ innovation projects a year across more than fifteen factories. Intelactory isn't a guess at what factories need; it's the tool I wished I'd had for the last decade.
