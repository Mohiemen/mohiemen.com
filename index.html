WHY I'M BUILDING THIS

I've done this work by hand, at scale, for a decade.

Every factory I've worked in has the same gap: production planning, quality tracking and sustainability reporting live in spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads and someone's memory. I've built the manual version of the fix — real-time dashboards, SOPs, innovation registers — inside more than fifteen factories. Intelactory is that fix, built once, as software.

FOUNDER · BOOTSTRAPPED

Intelactory: the intelligent factory operating system

Intelactory connects planning, production, quality, traceability and sustainability into one platform for textile and apparel manufacturers — an AI Control Tower that flags risk before it costs an order, full fibre-to-carton traceability, and compliance-ready reporting, built from the exact problems I've solved by hand on factory floors across Bangladesh.
